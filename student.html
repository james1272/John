Student Life at Levites Model College

At Levites Model College, we believe in creating a vibrant, inclusive environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally. Here’s a glimpse into student life and the resources available to you.

Student Resources

We provide various resources to support our students, including:

  • State-of-the-art classrooms and learning spaces.
  • Access to a comprehensive library with a wide range of books and digital resources.
  • Guidance and counseling services to support personal and academic growth.
  • Sports and extracurricular clubs to foster teamwork and creativity.
  • Study and career counseling to help students make informed decisions about their future.

Academic Achievements

Our students consistently excel in national and international exams. Here are some of our recent achievements:

  • Top 5 in the National Science Olympiad.
  • Winners of the Regional Debate Competition.
  • Outstanding performance in the Annual Mathematics Challenge.
  • Consistently high pass rates in external examinations.

Extracurricular Activities

At Levites Model College, we understand the importance of a well-rounded education. Students have access to a variety of extracurricular activities, including:

  • Drama and Performing Arts Club
  • Science and Technology Club
  • Sports Teams (Football, Basketball, Volleyball)
  • Music and Choir
  • Community Service Projects
