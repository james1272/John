Admissions

Welcome to Levites Model College! We are excited to have you join our community. Our admissions process is designed to ensure that students who share our vision for excellence and personal growth are welcomed into our school.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form.
  • Copies of the student’s birth certificate.
  • Most recent academic transcripts or report cards.
  • Two passport-sized photographs.
  • Recommendation letter from the previous school (if applicable).
  • Entrance examination (details provided upon application).

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Fill out the application form.
  2. Step 2: Submit the completed form along with the required documents to the school’s admissions office.
  3. Step 3: Schedule and take the entrance examination.
  4. Step 4: Attend an interview (if required).
  5. Step 5: Receive your admission decision and complete the enrollment process.

Entrance Examination

Our entrance examination evaluates students in core subjects such as Mathematics, English, and Science. The purpose is to assess the student's readiness for our curriculum. Details about the examination date and venue will be communicated upon submission of the application.
